Step 1 of 3 - Retrieve the accessory kit
Open the flavor shelf and take out the kit.
  • Locate the flavor shelf inside the machine
  • Remove the accessory kit
Step 2 of 3 - Verify kit contents
Check that all required parts are present.
  • 8 flavor connectors (set aside for later)
  • Drip tray components (bobber and grate)
  • Gen 1 gray grate, install now if includedoptionalSee the image below for reference
Not all kits include a Gen 1 gray grate. Skip that item if yours does not have one.
Step 3 of 3 - Install the drip tray
Assemble the drip tray components.
  • Assemble the drip tray bobber and grate
Optional: If you have a drain kit to install, complete that first before continuing. View the drain kit guide
Step 3 of 3 - Install the drip tray
Slot the drip tray into position.
  • Slot the drip tray into the dispense area
